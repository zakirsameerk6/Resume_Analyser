Job Title: Senior Data & Analytics Specialist
Company: CloudData Technologies
Location: Remote / New York, NY

Job Overview:
We are seeking an experienced Data Analyst / Analytics Specialist to join our enterprise insights team. The candidate will be responsible for creating modern business intelligence solutions, building scalable analytics pipelines, and collaborating with cross-functional leadership.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and deploy high-impact dashboards and reports using Power BI and Looker.
- Author and optimize complex SQL queries across cloud data warehouses such as Snowflake and PostgreSQL.
- Perform exploratory data analysis and statistical modeling using Python (Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn).
- Partner with data engineering teams to design ETL data pipelines using Apache Spark and Airflow.
- Deploy analytics models and cloud reporting infrastructure on AWS (S3, Redshift).
- Communicate technical insights clearly to non-technical stakeholders and executive leadership.
- Participate in Agile/Scrum standups and sprint planning.

Required Qualifications & Skills:
- 3+ years of professional experience in Data Analysis or Business Intelligence.
- Strong proficiency in SQL and relational databases (PostgreSQL, MySQL).
- Advanced expertise in Python programming for data manipulation (Pandas, NumPy).
- Deep experience in BI visualization tools (Power BI, Tableau, or Looker).
- Hands-on experience with Cloud platforms, particularly AWS.
- Familiarity with Big Data and pipeline technologies such as Snowflake and Apache Spark.
- Excellent Problem Solving and Communication skills.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or equivalent field.
